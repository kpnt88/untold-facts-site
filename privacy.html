Privacy Policy

Untold Facts is an automated video publishing tool for a single owner-operated TikTok account.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any personal data from end users. The app only uploads videos to the owner's own TikTok account via the official API.

Third Parties

Video content is uploaded to TikTok under TikTok's own privacy policy. No analytics or tracking is performed.
